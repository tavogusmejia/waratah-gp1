What this is and is not. It records what each submitted datasheet states. A blank is not stated on the sheet, which is not the same as not certified — several European makers here print no mark at all on a catalogue page while CE remains compulsory for them to sell at all. Where a sheet is silent, the answer is to ask the supplier for the Declaration of Conformity or the listing file, not to assume either way.

The register mixes European fixtures carrying CE and ENEC with American ones carrying UL and ETL. Which of the two the project actually needs depends on the jurisdiction, and nothing in the datasheets settles that — it is worth settling before the next order.
